Company Profile

Fujian Demao Fastener Co., Ltd

Fujian Demao Fastener Co., Ltd was established on June 4, 2018, covering an area of more than 20,000 square meters. The company is located at No. 6-1, Yacheng Town Industrial Park, Xiapu County, Ningde City, Fujian Province, China.

We focus on the research, development, production and sale of screws, bolts and related fastener products, serving customers across machinery manufacturing, furniture & building materials, and electronics industries, with products supplied to both domestic and overseas markets.
