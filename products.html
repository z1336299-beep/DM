Product Categories

Six Core Series

Photographed from our own product samples.

Hexagon Socket Screw Series

Socket head cap screws (cup head, flat cup, round cup) with hexagon internal drive, available in black oxide, nickel and zinc finishes.

Socket Set Screw Series

Headless set screws (DIN 913/914/915/916 flat, cone, dog and cup point) used to lock components in place without protruding.

Hexagon Head Screw Series

External hexagon head screws for high-torque assembly, common in machinery and structural connections.

Combination Screw Series

Cup head or round cup screws pre-assembled with flat and spring washers, saving assembly time and ensuring consistent clamping force.

Knitting Machine Screws

A dedicated line of hex socket, cross-slot and adjustment screws engineered specifically for knitting machine components.

Non-Standard Screw Series

Custom-engineered fasteners — including flat/countersunk heads, large flat heads and expansion screws — produced to customer drawings or samples.
